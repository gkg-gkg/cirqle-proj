Deal The deal shoppers see on Browse Deals — its title or brand name. Views How many times shoppers have opened this deal's page. Clicks How many times shoppers have clicked through to the brand's site. Claims How many shoppers have submitted a receipt claiming cashback on this deal. Revenue Total amount spent on receipts claimed against this deal. Cashback Total cashback you've paid shoppers on this deal. Referrals Pays a bonus to a shopper who refers a friend, and to the friend, once the friend's claim is approved. Off by default — you choose the amounts when you turn it on.

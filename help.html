How can we help?

Getting started

Sign up for free, browse our partner deals and shop as you normally would. Post about your purchase on Instagram, tagging both @cirqle and the brand, then upload your receipt. Once your claim is approved, the cashback clears into your wallet and you can withdraw it to your bank.
Completely free. No subscriptions, no membership fees, no tiers, and nothing is taken out of your cashback. Brands pay us to reach real customers who post about them, and we pass a share of that to you.
We work with brands across fashion, beauty, food, travel, tech, home and fitness — including Nike, ASOS, Boots, Gymshark, Deliveroo, Spotify and Airbnb. The full list, with every rate, is on the deals page.
You have to actually buy the thing, actually post about it, and tag both the brand and @cirqle. If you weren’t going to make the purchase anyway, the cashback isn’t worth it.

Cashback

Withdrawals

From your dashboard, choose Withdraw. Cashback is sent to your bank account via Stripe — you’ll verify your identity and add your bank details on Stripe’s own secure form, so we never see or store them.
Not at the moment. Bank transfer through Stripe is the only withdrawal method we support today.
The minimum is £5.00, because below that the transfer fees would eat most of the payout. There’s no maximum — withdraw as much as you have cleared.

How it works

1

Claim a deal yourself — buy, post about it on Instagram, and upload your receipt as normal.

2

Share your handle. A friend who buys through your post enters your @handle when they upload their receipt.

3

You both get paid — £1 to you, 50p to them, once their claim clears the 3-day hold.

4

It lands in your wallet alongside your cashback, and comes out in the same withdrawal.

The small print

✓

Your own claim for that deal has to be approved first — a referral only counts if you actually promoted the deal.

✓

It has to be your friend's first claim on that deal, and your post has to come before their purchase.

✓

You'll both need to have verified your identity for payouts, which you need to withdraw anything anyway.

✓

Not every deal offers it — the brand chooses, and funds it. Your dashboard says which of your referrals have paid.

How Cirqle works for brands

Set a cashback rate, fund it, and pay only when a real customer makes a real purchase and shares it. Here is the whole thing — onboarding, verification, and exactly what comes out of your budget.

Getting set up

Five steps, and none of them need anything from your engineering team. There is no pixel to install, no feed to sync and no change to your checkout — Cirqle works off the receipt your till already prints, which is why an in-store purchase counts exactly the same as an online one.

1

Talk to us — a 20-minute call covering your categories, your margins and what a sensible cashback rate looks like for you. We set your merchant account up from there.

2

Upload a reference receipt — one clean receipt from your own till. This is the most important thing you give us: every member receipt claimed against you is compared to it, down to the store details and the layout. Without it we cannot tell your receipts from anyone else’s, so campaigns do not go live until it is on file.

3

Create a campaign — set the cashback rate, any minimum spend, the category and how long it runs. The rate is published on the deal, so shoppers see it before they buy and there is nothing to negotiate afterwards.

4

Fund the wallet — add credit in the Billing tab. Cashback and referral bonuses are paid out of that balance, so you always know your ceiling: when the wallet is empty, nothing more can be owed.

5

Go live — your deal is listed on Browse Deals and in its categories. Most brands are live inside 48 hours of the first call.

What happens when someone claims

You are paying for purchases, so the purchase has to be real. Every claim goes through the same checks, in the same order, and a claim that fails one never reaches your budget. You are not charged for it, and you do not have to spot it yourself.

The receipt is matched to yours

Every uploaded receipt is read and compared against the reference receipt you gave us at onboarding — store name, address, phone number, VAT number, and the visual format of the receipt itself. A receipt that did not come from one of your tills does not look like one of your receipts.

Compared against your own reference receipt
The arithmetic is recomputed

Line items are added against the subtotal, and subtotal plus tax against the total — in code, not by a model. This is what catches fabricated receipts: generators reproduce how a receipt looks and then routinely get its sums wrong. We also check the purchase fits the campaign — a plausible item, a sane date, and at least your minimum spend.

Fails the maths, fails the claim
No receipt is paid twice

A byte-identical upload is rejected at the door. A re-saved, cropped or re-photographed copy is caught too, because we fingerprint what the image looks like, not just the file. The same check runs across accounts, so one receipt cannot quietly fund claims from several profiles.

Exact and near-duplicate detection
The post has to name you

We read the accounts tagged in the post and check them against the campaign being claimed. A post tagging a different Cirqle brand is rejected outright — you never pay for content that promoted someone else. The post also has to stay up until the claim is checked, so the content you paid for is content that actually ran.

Brand tag matched to your campaign
Bought audiences are scored down

Accounts are scored on follower-to-following ratio, engagement rate, and consistency across recent posts. Engagement far below what an account that size should draw reads as bought followers; far above it, or near-identical post after post, reads as an engagement pod. You are reaching real people, not a follower count someone bought.

Account quality scored on every claim
Referral bonuses cannot be farmed

A referral bonus needs both people identity-verified through Stripe, and is blocked when two accounts share bank or identity details — compared as hashed fingerprints, so the details themselves are never held. The referrer’s post has to predate the friend’s purchase, and it only counts on that friend’s first claim for the deal. One person with two accounts earns nothing.

Stripe identity verification on both sides

Behind the automated checks there is a human backstop. The same deal claimed over and over by one member, a claim filed minutes after an account was created, one image appearing across multiple accounts, one post backing claims from several accounts — all of it is flagged and reviewed by a person before any money moves. Held, not paid.

We are straight about the one thing this is not: it is not image forensics. A skilfully edited real receipt whose numbers still add up will read as fine to the automated checks — which is exactly why the duplicate detection, the tag matching, the account scoring and the human review exist alongside it. No single check carries the weight, and that is the point.

What you actually pay for

Performance only. No CPM, no click charge, no spend on impressions that went nowhere — money leaves your wallet when a verified purchase has been made and shared, and not before.

Cashback
Your published rate on what the customer actually spent, paid once their claim has passed every check.
Referral bonuses
Optional, and off unless you switch them on. £1 to the sharer and 50p to the friend who bought, from the same wallet.
Failed claims: nothing
A claim that fails verification costs you nothing. Not deducted, not held against your balance, not your problem.

What you see in your dashboard

Your merchant portal is where all of this is visible — not a monthly PDF, but the live state of every campaign and every claim against it.

Overview
Revenue attributed, return, claims, cost, cashback given and what is still pending — alongside deals, views and clicks.
Customers
Who bought, what they spent, and where each claim has got to in verification.
Posts
The actual content your campaign produced, tied back to the purchase it belongs to.
Referrals
The referral chain — who shared with whom, and which bonuses were paid or blocked.
Merchant profile
Your campaigns, your rates, and the reference receipt every claim is matched against.
Billing
Wallet balance, top-ups and membership. Your ceiling is whatever you have funded.

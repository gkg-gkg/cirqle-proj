5Questions

Quick answers before you decide

What does it cost?

It works as profit sharing. You give cashback and referral bonuses only once a sale has been processed and verified, plus a small platform fee. We'll go through the numbers with you before you sign up.

Do I need any new equipment?

No. Customers upload their receipts on their own phones. Nothing changes at your till.

My Instagram is quite small. Does that matter?

No. Customers share you with their own friends, on social media or in-person, so you reach people who don't follow you yet.

How do you stop fake receipts?

Every receipt goes through checks: we read it, match it to your shop, look for duplicates and screen for fraud, and a person reviews it. Rejected receipts cost you nothing.

I already run offers. Do I keep them?

That's up to you. Any offers you run now can stay as they are. Cirqle is about getting your customers to bring their friends in.

How do I get started?

Tap Apply to partner below, or just tell us when we're in. We'll set up your deal with you.

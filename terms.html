3. How Cirqle works

Cirqle is a cashback marketplace. In outline:

  • You browse deals from brands on Cirqle. We are onboarding our first brands now: a deal marked as an example is there to show how a listing will look, and can’t be claimed.
  • You make a qualifying purchase from the brand (subject to that deal's terms).
  • You follow our Instagram account, @cirqle.co.uk.
  • You post about it on Instagram, tagging the brand / our account as the deal requires.
  • You upload your receipt as proof of purchase.
  • We verify the claim, and confirmed cashback is added to your Cirqle wallet.

Some brands also let you log a repeat visit: once you have claimed one of their deals, you can upload a later receipt from the same brand without posting again. A repeat visit earns the amount that brand has set for one, which is lower than the deal's posted rate, and it cannot be used to name someone who referred you.

Brands fund the cashback you earn from them, and in return they can see your activity with that brand — including your Instagram handle, how often you have claimed, what your receipts totalled, and who referred you to them. They never see your name, email, receipt images, or anything about your activity with any other brand. Section 5 of our Privacy Policy sets this out in full, and it is worth reading before you claim.

If withdrawals are ever temporarily unavailable, your wallet will say so, and we may hold a withdrawal while we complete identity or fraud checks. Your wallet balance is a record of cashback owed to you under these terms; it is not a bank account and does not earn interest.

7. Referrals & the leaderboard

If you tell a friend about something you bought and they buy it too, you can both earn a referral bonus. There is no link to send: your friend types your Instagram handle into their own claim. For the bonus to be paid:

  • you must have posted about that deal, and your post must come before their purchase;
  • it must be your friend’s first claim on that deal; and
  • your friend’s claim must be approved and clear its 3-day hold.

The brand sets and funds the bonus for each deal. It is at least £1 to you and 50p to your friend, and the deal shows the amount. You can’t refer yourself, including from a second account, and both of you are identity-checked by Stripe before either is paid.

The referral leaderboard is optional and off until you join it. It runs in three-month seasons, ranked by referrals settled in that season. The top ten at the end of a season each win the cash prize shown on the leaderboard page. A season’s table is final once its standings have been confirmed, and we may remove anyone who breaks these terms.

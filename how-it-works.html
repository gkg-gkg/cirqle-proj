The two ways to share

Sharing is the step that earns, so it is worth knowing both routes. You can use either, or both on the same purchase.

Share with your followers

Post your buy on Instagram, tag @cirqle and the brand, then upload the receipt. We match the post to your handle and the receipt to the deal.

Earns the deal's published rate
Share with your friends

Send them your link. When a friend buys through your post and enters your handle on their receipt, you both get paid once their first claim on that deal is approved.

£1 to you, 50p to them

Been back since?

Some brands also accept a repeat visit. Once you have claimed one of their deals, a later receipt from the same brand can be logged on its own — no post needed. It pays a smaller fixed amount that the brand sets, and the deal page tells you when it is available.

Why brands pay for this

Brands spend heavily on influencer marketing. Cirqle points that money at real customers instead: you buy something you were going to buy, you tell people about it honestly, and the brand pays a commission. Your cashback is a share of that commission — which is why it is free to join and why the rate depends on the deal.

Still got questions? The help centre covers the details, and the terms spell out when a claim can be withheld.
